# Mixed Step 4: exact run and collar transfers
For a finite row let W,E be its physical strict-west and strict-east integers, least significant bits nearest center. Let ν denote the 2-adic valuation; set ν(0)=∞. The remaining lifetime of its current center color, including the present tick, is
* black: 1+ν(3W+2);
* white, E>0: 1+ν(3W+(−2)^ν(E));
* white, E=0: 1+ν(W).

Proof. While center is black its left head must be 0 to continue black. The west comparator 0101… (outwards) is invariant under constant black input. Its 2-adic integer is −2/3. Under a clamped boundary the first unequal bit of two west tapes at index i moves to i−1 per update because the furthest west argument is XOR-permutive, hence exactly i ticks later their left heads differ. If center is white, its right head under held white is 0 until the nearest east black arrives, followed by ones forever. If b=ν(E), the west tape that perpetually matches that right head under held white is X_b: indices <b are zero, index b is 1, all greater i have bit 1−(i mod 2). Indeed the white evolution H(x)=(x>>1) XOR (x OR (x<<1)) sends X_b to X_(b−1) for b>0 and fixes X_0, by inspection of the interface at b and the stationary exterior. Its 2-adic value is −(−2)^b/3; the entire east-blank comparator is zero. The first unequal west-head tick makes the center flip on the following tick. This proves the formulas including truncated tails. Run `python run_coupling.py` to replay the singletons through 500; the white/black predictions depend only on the initial row.

For arbitrary concatenations of blocks 0^n11, n=6 or n≥8, the exact controlled transition of six east bits has a finite absorbing family after two blocks. In that family the low 2-bit prefix has a 1 (east rise delay 0 or 1); once the lowest bit is 1 every next block has lowest bit 1 as well. Therefore along any such infinite concatenation the rise date is eventually constant; a periodic concatenation gives a periodic masked right trace, and is incompatible with the actual single seed by the frozen masked-right theorem. The finite relation proof is `family_certificate.py`; `white_window_singleton.py` tests the local reduction against both actual singleton controls.

For any temporal input of nine black ticks the same 6-bit collar locks into east pair 01 (ordered spatially from center outward); on further black the pair stays fixed. The worst-case time-to-lock rank is computed by `black_lock.py`. Each further 9 black ticks produces 2 more checker bits by translation. Any periodic wall with all black runs ≥9 consequently has east delay 1 on every white run, independently of its white lengths, and likewise yields the forbidden periodic mask. Neither family includes all mixed words.

A checker right prefix 01 repeated to even length d under any common subsequent center shields the right head for 2d ticks. The outer 1 of the prefix remains closed until a disturbance arrives from the west, after which there is the ordinary return flight. The 9-black reset thus covers periodic words with a black anchor B and arbitrary complementary subword of length C ≤ 4 floor(B/9); chek: checker_echo.py. At the other extreme, for mixed clocks with every white run ≥19 and arbitrary intervening black lengths, the exact 14-bit right collar reaches a 10101 prefix immediately before every black run in at most three complete blocks (white_family.py). From that prefix the next white rise delay is 2,0,4,0,1 according as the preceding black length is 1,2,3,4,or ≥5.

For *every periodic mixed center word* w and every putative periodic visible mask v supported on w's whites (use a common period), set L=S w XOR w XOR v, with S the cyclic time shift. Starting from the two time columns w,L, continue west by (a,b)→(b,S b XOR (b OR a)). This finite transformation makes the strict west slice eventually periodic in space. Write its 2-adic value at the chosen phase as A/B, B odd. Under actual center drive w, the true west head and the comparator head first differ exactly at ν₂(B W−A), where W is the whole finite strict-west integer: the first unequal bit marches toward the head one place per tick under a common clamped center. Because on a common center the mask and west-head differences coincide, this is exactly the time to first exit from v. The same statement applies to a finite center-match window with right censored exit. The rational A/B cannot be a nonnegative integer for a genuine mixed periodic pair, since a nonzero finite west row grows its greatest occupied index by one each tick and is not periodic. In particular 0001/0010 gives −8/127; 0011/0100 gives −16/127; 011/100 and 011/000 give −424/1365 and −53/1365. See periodic_reflector.py.

With the complete right-edge-normalized finite row X and J(X)=X XOR ((2X) OR (4X)), put P(X)=X & ~(X>>1) & ~(X>>2). In ordinary integer arithmetic J(X)+X=8P(X). Partition the ones into clusters, cutting at gaps of at least two zeros. On each cluster from bit l to k, J keeps the lowest one, complements all higher bits through k, and appends two ones; its value plus the input segment is 2^(k+3). Thus the sources P are separated by at least three. In physical coordinates a western cluster endpoint at site x is C(x)(1−C(x−1))(1−C(x−2))=C_t(x)C_(t+1)(x)(1−C_(t+2)(x)). Hence the same marker is 3-separated in physical time as well as space.

Splitting the cluster identity at the physical center gives 2W'+W+b=8P(W)+2MM'(1−2M''), b=W mod 2. At fixed lag Q of a common center tail the signed differences therefore obey 2δW'+δW+δb=8δP, with δb supported on white phases. For the east integer E let r=E mod 2 and U(E)=E&~(E>>1). Its exact mirrored balance is 2E'+3E+r=2[U(E)+4P(E)]−2M+4Mr'. This follows from f(a,b,c)−f(c,b,a)=bc−ab over integers, telescoping along the half. The two supports U(E),4P(E) do not overlap. Every entire normalized-left front X with X_t(0)=1 and X_t(j)=0 for j<0 has *no eventually spatially periodic right tail* in any phase. Its local rule is L(X)_j=X_(j−2) XOR (X_(j−1) OR X_j). First, every lane is an all-time dyadic clock by induction: a nonzero preceding lane resets once per period, while a constantly zero preceding lane gives an XOR integrator, doubling at most. The possible first-eight phases are exactly 0x13 and 0x7b (low bit is marked site 0); each contains an ordinary inverse synchronizer, as in LeftResetCollar. If L(Y) is p-periodic on [c,∞), scan Y from infinitely far right: each pair of predecessor bits is a deterministic function of the pair to its right and the target bit. At a given p-phase every pair state therefore lies on a cycle of the finite four-state backwards transformation (an infinite path in the inverse of a finite function consists of cycle states). Thus Y is periodic from c−2, with period at most 4p. If the target's repeated period contains a synchronizer, every such predecessor has period p. Rewind any purported right-periodic tail until its cutoff is nonpositive. The marked eight bits then supply a synchronization word in that tail, repeated arbitrarily far east. At every subsequent rewind the cutoff drops by two and the period no longer grows. Eventually the right-periodic tail includes 0 and its negative-period translate, contradiction. In particular no finite phase occurs. A second proof for finite phases uses cofinally many permanently white lanes at all necessary doublings, followed by RecurrentTailReset.no_vacuum_tail. recurrent_front.py rejects all 501 actual finite R30 states by a concrete recurrent-prefix discrepancy; R90 admits all 501 via invertible linear prefixes.

Finite consequence and arbitrary-complement white anchor: given any C≥1, there is a computable H0(C) such that repeated runs of at least H0(C) whites separated by *arbitrary mixed pieces* of length 1..C containing black have a right mask determined event-by-event by the intervening pieces after one cycle. In particular every periodic word with a single such long anchor and complementary length ≤C is excluded. Here is a sufficient bound. K=9(C+2), D=2C+2K+4. All entire marked front states have a 1 in [D,B(D)] for some finite B(D) by compactness and the no-finite-front theorem. T(B) is a uniform number of ticks required for all marked B-prefixes to enter their finite recurrent orbits. Take H0=max(K+C+B(D)+3,T(B)). In a white run of length H write a for distance in the strict east word to its first 1, with a=∞ allowed. If a≤H−K, the head R stays 1 in the last K ticks, writing a checker of length ≥2C+3 (nine black inputs write two). This determines the entire next complement's mask and next delay from that complement; the checker beyond its lightcone guarantees next delay ≤C+1. If a≥H+2C+2, a blank collar survives to the complement; its first black input launches a packet into vacant space whose farthest black survives to the next white, so next delay ≤C. In the remaining case, compare with a free CA from a normalized marked front at position a+1 and vacuum to its west. Before time min(a+1,H) the free center and actual held white agree; at the end of a complement of length c≤C, perturbation due to its inputs or center clamping extends no farther east than C+K. The free front lies at coordinate a+1−H−c in [−K−C,2C+2]. Its B-prefix is in the recurrent class, hence some free 1 in normalized [D,B] lies strictly beyond the disturbance and within distance B+2C+2 of center (using c≥1 and the strict critical-case upper bound, a+1−H−c ≤2C). For varying complement lengths use H0=max(K+2C+B(D)+3,T(B)); for a fixed complement of length C the displayed K+C+B+3 suffices. Thus the next run is in the good regime. This is an arbitrary-complement sufficient condition, not a deduction that every periodic word has such an anchor. white_anchor.py gives conservative H0 for a fixed C: C=1,2,4,8,16 yield 370,538,866,1482,2762. Its actual-front transfer has 1,378 physical R30 examples without error, and 9,591 R90 errors. The overall bridge for mixed clocks without long anchors is open.

Then t−ell(t) tends to +infinity. Indeed if ell(t_i)≥t_i−D with t_i→∞, the west reflector A/B fixes all these W_(t_i) bits up to index t_i−D−1. For two actual rows separated by Q≥2, all their unequal spacetime cells in any forward time tail belong to ONE forced swapping component. A swap at an unequal cell means exchange its two donor labels. At a site where the two OR arguments differ across donors, two varying arguments must swap together (otherwise whether the OR outputs differ changes). When the left XOR argument alone varies or the OR alone varies, the outgoing varying bit must inherit that swap; when both vary and cancel, the left swap must equal the effective OR swap. These are exact equality constraints, hence satisfying all of them makes the two partially swapped diagrams true evolutions, and conversely. From any unequal site (t,x), follow its mandatory left-XOR use to (t+1,x+1) if this site differs. If not, the OR at (t,x+1),(t,x+2) must differ, so it ties the swap to a differing site one or two places east at the SAME t. In coordinates j=older right edge−x, the first move retains j and the second strictly lowers j. Therefore the path eventually rides one fixed positive-difference ray forever. The older edge j=0 is the unique such ray: for 0<j<Q or j=Q use the two genuine colors of every positive-depth ray. For j>Q the continuous older and younger clocks have dyadic periods; at an older return to phase 0, its first ceil(j/2) ticks are white, and this is longer than the maximum single-color run ceil((j−Q)/2) on the younger ray. The needed initial/maximal-run facts are RayRunBound.sharp_initial_white/no_constant. Thus every swap is forced to equal the same older-edge swap. A path can bypass a matched center from west to east only through a same-tick horizontal jump −1→1, exactly a differing white mask. Arbitrary-lag first returns: write A(t)=(M(t),g(t)), and r(n) for the later starting index of the first duplicate length-n A-factor. Then r(n)−n→+∞ for the actual singleton, unconditionally. Sharper inverse folds. Two distinct global spatially periodic predecessors of the same target have disjoint black supports: one common black at j fixes both old j,j−1 from the shared target bit j, and the ordinary inverse scan agrees indefinitely west, hence globally by periodicity. Their pair states at every two adjacent sites must differ, so their union has no adjacent gaps. If three predecessors exist, the three states on any adjacent pair must be exactly 00,01,10 because supports are disjoint. Were a target symbol 0, its predecessor bit at j−1 would equal the OR of the two-site pair in each donor, forcing two donors black there. Thus a threefold fiber occurs only for the uniform black target, whose predecessors are the three shifts of (001)^∞. Consequently every period increase in undoing a nonconstant target is a doubling, and a triple increase can occur only once in a backward chain. If Y has period 2p and maps to a p-periodic target, color the union of Y and shift_p Y by the owning donor. A simultaneous zero occurs: without one, every neighboring pair must keep the same owner because equal output with the preceding union bit 1 requires unequal OR, contradicting the swapping shift. Just east of a simultaneous zero the next two positions have opposite owners (both ORs equal and the next site occupied), so one contains an isolated black in its owner's row. Translation swaps the donors, so Y contains an isolated black, making its own inverse unique by the spatial 010 synchronizer. A tripled predecessor of uniform black has isolated ones as well. Thus successive inverse increases cannot be adjacent and after k steps the period is ≤H=3p·2^ceil(k/2).
