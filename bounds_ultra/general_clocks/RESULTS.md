# General periodic clocks: right language versus a marked finite west

Consider phase-zero clamped clocks 01, 011, 0011, 00101. A strobe is one complete P-step word. Its observable is the chronological vector of actual x=1 bits at **every white**; it is not the number of paths in the right graph. In Rule 30 the prescribed west head is c(t+1) XOR (c(t) OR R1(t)). Rows west of the center therefore follow a triangular left-permutive reconstruction from the observable. Left and right depths are physical, nearest first.

The spatial mature-prefix counts at six widths are in spatial.txt. These are rooted prefix languages; isolated ratios and slopes do not establish their asymptotic growth rates. The temporal width-18 variable hosts have sizes 2110/5123/6186/17062, respectively; all but 6186 are absorbing. Starting from all states in a host, entropy.py enumerates successive genuine radius-one updates with free exterior and determinizes by white-read labels. This computes the full sofic language of that finite host; extension to a single infinite eastern half-row is a separate condition. The exact finite-host entropy is the base-two logarithm of the spectral radius of the JSON adjacency (counting each label once) divided by P. Numerical values, in clock order: 0.063533, 0.109641, 0.095618, 0.118336 bits per physical tick. Counts of length-31 words: 84, 6404, 18479, 6292109. The known width-25 58203 host projected via its verified 105-state gap grammar has 0.063307 bits per physical tick and the same first 31 counts as its width-18 counterpart. These positive entropies do not require any individual right trace to have positive entropy. The real infinite orbit, if it belongs to one of these hosts, gets an upper bound, not a lower bound.

## Marked-west restriction: uniform cap on a periodic read island

Fix a repeating observable v allowed by the chosen host. Form its full periodic clock c (period L) and its periodic west columns by exact lateral propagation: first h=Sc XOR (c OR v), then (a,b) -> (b,Sb XOR (a OR b)), with cyclic time shift S and black-observation coordinates ignored. Finiteness of column pairs gives a transient of o spatial sites followed by a periodic tail E in every time phase. Assume E's phases contain a black at least every g+1 sites. If, on reading the clean E phase, the R30 inverse recurrence x[j+1]=E[j] XOR (x[j] OR x[j-1]) always synchronizes to the previous tail phase within K sites regardless of the entering pair, set C=K+1. This hypothesis is a deterministic four-state test. For ANY finite marked initial west and an eventually c-driven center, suppose a future observable match lasts h ticks starting at age t. Then limsup h/t <= (C-1)/(C+1) as t goes to infinity, at matching reference phases.

Indeed rewind the comparator s steps through arbitrary actual past heads and prescribed centers, starting at the exact comparator row at t. Its deviations from the phase-correct ether remain below o+O(K)+C s: after the last changed target symbol the two-bit scan has K further sites to synchronize; a new boundary defect obeys the same bound when the old defect dies out. By triangularity, the new comparator and the true west now agree in their first s+h sites. But at age t-s the finite west is zero from depth t-s+O(1) onward. Choose s=floor((t-O(o+K))/(1+C)), wholly above onset when t is large. The comparator is clean beyond the actual support, and a black within g more sites forces h+s<=t-s+O(g+1). This uses the genuine marked past at every earlier age, not the entropy or rarity of a codeword. A different seed width changes the additive constant only.

periodic_islands.py found this test passes for observed-strobe cycles: 01:(0,1,0,0,0) with C=26 (the old sharper graph certificate gives 27/35 on the ratio), 011:(0,1,1) with C=36 giving 35/37, 00101:(0) with C=24 giving 23/25. In the 0011 host the elementary accepted constant regimes (1), (3) give the same 7-site ether family with an ambiguous inverse; the single-predecessor hypothesis fails. The multi-preimage test in inverse_tail_atlas.py keeps all spatial cyclic branches rather than falsely assuming the center fixes the tail. These are conditional ceilings for a single periodic observable. For arbitrary q the needed long islands are not established.

## Rule 90 exact matched adversary

Full Rule90 clamped channel is right-permutive: successive R1 readings can be specified one at a time by the unique newly reached initial eastern bit. All white-word vectors are thus free; projected rate (#whites)/P, and mature spatial prefix count 2^n (right-permutivity also makes every output prefix attainable). Its inverse two-bit scan is a permutation for every target symbol, so no Rule30 synchronizer exists in this rule.

Stronger: each of the four clocks is attainable on a full actually evolving Rule90 row with origin marked, strict west vacuum, and eventually periodic original east. Set C(0)=1, C(1+t)=clock(t). Over GF(2), set y=z(1+y^2)=sum_{k>=1} z^(2^k-1). The walk Green function from spatial site j>=0 to the origin is y^j: at j=0 the coefficient series is one by central-binomial parity, and the positive half satisfies the walk recursion, with the finite-propagation triangular condition fixing its solution. Hence initial strict-west-zero right series S(x)=C(x/(1+x^2)) produces C itself. The actual neighbor series is R1=(C-1)/z+y C, since y^{-1}=1/z+y. Thus its individual white-read complexity is O_P(n), although the full clamped channel has positive entropy. For a window lying strictly between successive dyadic breakpoints (including a fixed O(P) onset collar at each), R1 is P-periodic. At most O(n) starts precede the first breakpoint >2n; thereafter a window can meet at most one such collar, with finitely many periodic phase/mask choices. controls.py replayed each actual, unconstrained rule for 501 checked ages, including direct R1 checks. Original R90 east spatial primitive periods are 6/5/12/17 by clock order. These controls use their own actual Rule90 dynamics, never a Rule30 host.

The supplied r30fast runtime is a local dependency for generating spatial.txt and the width-18 DFAs. The included JSON DFAs can be analyzed without that runtime; no supplied archive source or binary is redistributed here.

The 0011 single-step obstruction is repaired with the shortest nonconstant label cycle of its width-18 projection, (1,1,1,1,1,3,3). The calendar labels 1 and 3 are the two-bit white strings 01 and 11. At 28 physical ticks, periodic_spatial.cpp finds lateral transient 6808, spatial period 363832, maximum gap 17, and exact maximum inverse convergence 75 over every site and all 28 temporal phases (a complete two-pass four-state DP). Thus C=76 and limsup island/age <=75/77. This does not require unique spatial ancestors for the simpler constant clock cycles that failed. For the simpler cycles, even using the exact 8-backstep inverse with all boundary-allowed histories and physically reachable entering states leaves nonmatching far tails.

## Actual finite-tape support versus the whole right-host language

There is a period-blind, nested ordinary-integer statistic, not a count of arbitrary q paths. Given an admitted strobe word u of length k, the clamped western decoder yields one integer W(u) in [0,2^(Pk)). Successive west-head samples form a 2-adic triangular bijection: the earliest h discrepancy at time j corresponds precisely to the earliest physical W discrepancy at position j. Extending a q prefix appends west bits to W(u). Hence m(k)=min W(u) across all length-k observable factors is nondecreasing. If m(k) tends to infinity then the host accepts no infinite trace of any finite west tape. Conversely, if it stays bounded, compactness of the finite branching labeled tree gives an admitted infinite q with finite W. Such a trace is a joint west/finite-host boundary history, not automatically a true eastern infinite-ray realization. Unlike an entropy deficit, divergence suffices by itself.

minfinite.py compiles minfinite.cpp and enumerates the projected *distinct* factor words. It further forward-replays every minimizing W under the clamped west dynamics. Final columns in minwest_*.txt are clock, strobes, physical ticks, distinct words, bit length of the least west tape, hexadecimal tape, and chronological q labels. For 01/011/0011/00101, horizons 126/126/124/125 give minima of 116/112/110/104 bits. There were respectively 1400/78897/18479/536067 admitted words. These are finite-support lower bounds, not divergence. True matched R90 controls have a one-bit strict west at age one and permit all future required white readings with genuine eastern evolution, so no analogous divergence occurs there.

The overlap relaxation lateral_envelope.py slides height-6 or height-8 two-column temporal blocks indefinitely westward, retaining phase and overlapping all but one tick. At height 8 its eventual fixed set, independently in each of the four right hosts, consists of 2916 words at each phase, attained at western depths 40/43/38/29. Every sampled phase of the exact regularized normalized marked-rim arrival belongs to that fixed set. Those 2916 are the total number of unrestricted genuine Rule30 two-column words of height 8 by direct initial-cone enumeration; at height 6 the analogous count is 496. This demonstrates where a fixed-height relaxation loses the marked obstruction. The exact unlimited-temporal factor at a fixed western site need not have this unrestricted language: the fixed wall and the initial bits of the intervening finite strip reconstruct the center's masked q. Consequently entropy of each *single* fixed western temporal column equals that of its driving q factor for an individual orbit closure or a sofic ensemble with known periodic wall (initial strip costs at most 2^(d-1) choices, independent of observation length); the converse uses finite lookahead. Histories in the different finite-height overlap relaxations cannot be silently identified.
