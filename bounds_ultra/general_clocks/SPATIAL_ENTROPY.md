# The spatial entropy of a mature Rule 30 right prefix is at most 0.919228 bits/site

**Uniform statement.** Fix an arbitrary prescribed center history, periodic or not. At time zero consider the set of width-n right words Y(1),...,Y(n) with compatible histories for the past n ticks. At every tick the boundary at x=n+1 may be refreshed arbitrarily; only the local equations at x=1,...,n and the specified center are required. Its cardinality is at most the initial component of A^n 1 for the nine-state matrix specified below. In particular its limsup log₂(cardinality)/n is at most log₂ λ = 0.919227958... < 1, where λ = 1.891103020... is the Perron root of x⁵−2x⁴+x³−2x²+2x−2. The bound holds for each phase of every P and the mature width-n graph; it is a spatial result, not the white-observer entropy.

**The independent sample and its pivots.** Choose the full row at time −n i.i.d. fair, and run true Rule 30 forward to zero. Any queried spacetime cell at (x,t), −n≤t≤0, equals X(−n,x−t−n) XOR some function of initial cells *strictly to the east* of x−t−n. This is the unique extremal west influence and follows by induction from left permutivity. Any finite preassigned cell set with distinct pivots x−t has independent fair values: condition on all nonpivot initial variables and solve the pivot bits in descending pivot order for any desired outputs. The conclusion holds for a sequence of adaptive queries whose positions are chosen from earlier answers and have distinct pivots on each branch. Indeed for any fixed transcript the locations on that branch are fixed; the event that this branch occurs is exactly the prescribed joint cell event. This proof does not presume disjoint lightcones or that prior supports exclude later pivots in the query order. In particular the n current right bits are uniform as a word.

**Decode a past-center bit from a spatial suffix.** For a target y at x and unknown old pair (a,b) at (x+1,x), the old pair at (x,x−1) is f_y(a,b)=(b, y XOR (a OR b)). Start a right-to-left scan with all four pairs possible. If the possible set becomes a singleton, all previous-row possibilities agree on that pair. The known target bits to its west, down to x=1, now force the entire previous row between x=0 and that pair, hence the past center. This is a certificate valid for *every* compatible past, not a random-past assertion. Nothing is assumed beyond the known right endpoint, even at the first spatial cut.

**Probe schedule and matrix.** At layer t=−r, start with r=0 and largest unqueried right pivot s=n. Keep current inverse-pair possibilities S, initially all four. The pivots already used are 1,...,r for decoded centers and s+1,...,n for earlier probes. The current row is known on 1,...,s−r, except that within one unsuccessful scan it is the old known target with S accumulated from the cells just east of this range. Read the actual target cell x=s−r, t=−r and decrease s by one; this samples the unused pivot s_old. Replace S by f_y(S). If it is not a singleton, continue left on that same layer. If it is a singleton, the previous row is now forced through x=0 and at least up to x_old−1. Test its center against the prescribed value, using the next unused left pivot r+1; if incorrect, reject. After a correct test increment r, reset S to all four, and begin the next scan at x_old−2 on the newly decoded previous layer. If the n-pivot budget ends immediately after a final successful probe, no further comparison is needed. The centers read are precisely the consecutive prescribed past bits −1,−2,... . Any compatible current spatial prefix passes all tests. Running the probes on the full random sample always agrees with the determinate bits computed from its current spatial prefix.

A passing completed n-bit transcript uses exactly n distinct fair pivots; therefore the survival probability equals e₀ᵀ A^n 1 / 2^n. Multiplication by the 2^n possible current right words proves the bound. Exact equality with the mature set is not claimed. The surviving-path bound is strict already at n=5. In particular A has spectral radius strictly less than two, since it is irreducible, every row has at most two arcs and the check row has one.

**Where the marked proof still has to act.** This is stronger than a finite measured slope. It still bounds a *class* of possible spatial prefixes, so membership of the actual finite marked orbit is not excluded. On an actual age-t right tape, starting the reverse probe at its true east tip x=t uses pivots t−j relative to this cut, or original positions −j. Each probe increments the normalized east-rim depth j by exactly one even when the time layer changes.
