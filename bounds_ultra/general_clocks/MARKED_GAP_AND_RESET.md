# Closing arbitrary factors onto cyclic clocks and locating true inverse ambiguity

Let a genuine marked finite-west cut of width a lie in a recurrent right-prefix host, with fixed periodic center. Every strobe prefix of length N physical ticks can be completed, from its terminal state back to its starting state, by at most B further physical ticks (B depends on this finite host, not N). Repeat the resulting loop. Its outgoing white observations give a temporally periodic strict-west completion by the exact westward recurrence. At the chosen temporal phase, its first N west bits coincide with the true tape at the cut: a difference at depth j first changes the west-head word at tick j−1. Thus at a black of depth d=a this completion has a spatial zero gap g immediately west of the black with g≥N−a. For a loop of physical length L=N+B' define cost min[d+max(0,L−g)] over its black depths and all time phases. These loops have cost ≤2a+B. One may substitute primitive strobe periods without increasing cost. An infinite sequence of bounded primitive periods would make the original infinite q periodic, already excluded by the marked-west two-column theorem. Conversely, periodic loops with lengths tending to infinity and bounded cost yield, by a subsequence and compactness, an infinite admissible q in the finite host whose west tape really is finite and nonzero: pass to a fixed depth d, clock phase and near-center word first, then let the following zero gap diverge. This last statement does not assert a full right-half realization.

periodic_gap_cost.py evaluates this capped statistic on primitive label cycles of the four temporal DFAs. At longest enumerated primitive physical periods 68,66,76,70 the minimum costs were 64,65,75,62. These are finite computations, not divergence. cyclic_gap.cpp shows why dropping the right-wall anchoring is not justified: at L=9..11 arbitrary temporal-periodic right data can give transient west gaps 13,13,13 already. The fixed physical domain model for Rule90 is its entirely free right channel.

For the finite-parent test let W be one nonzero ordinary west word, near center first. Given the preceding center and west head, the old row is computed outward by f_y(a,b)=(b,y XOR (a OR b)). In its remaining all-zero target suffix, the previous row is finite if and only if the entering pair is 00: 00 is fixed and all other pairs end in 11. The four possible earlier heads/centers passing this test form a 4-bit state computed by scanning the target from its outer mark to the center using inverse image under each f_y, starting from {00}. The true left-normalized mark has the alternating low-eight patterns 0x13/0x7b after age 2 (apply the normalized update Z→4Z XOR (2Z OR Z)). At all ages ≥8 its outermost 6 or 8 bits alone send {00} back to all four states. Thus every true west tape at age ≥8 has a finite immediate predecessor even after arbitrarily specifying both prior center and head. This is a rooted, rule-specific supply of alternative pasts, not an alternative singleton history: the old words need not be the true tape one tick earlier. A greedy corridor of successive common four-pair resets certifies arbitrary earlier (center,head) choices over 9,17,29,41,45 ticks at ages 100,200,300,400,500 of the true singleton. R90 never has universal one-step finite parenthood. finite_west_parents.py also runs the four independently initialized clamped stress patterns.

For a complementary restriction, forbid every four-pair synchronizer in each of H consecutive spatial rows. Reading the bottom input row east to west, insert its actual neighboring Rule30 outputs into the higher rows with one-cell delays. The parser carries one mask per row and the last two spatial symbols per intermediate row; an empty history uses partial buffers. Reject as soon as any mask is singleton. The accepted finite words are exactly those whose genuine H-row finite cone has no synchronizer at any layer. At H=1 and 2 the spatial recurrent graphs have branching components. H=3 gives 83 reachable states and just four recurrent SCCs, ALL simple cycles: uniform 0, uniform 1 (two presentations), and rotations of the five-site ether 00111. The 5-cycle has exits to a uniform cycle, never conversely. Hence an entire triple of such rows has zero spatial entropy; every full spatial input is periodic or has one bounded transition from its periodic right tail to its periodic left tail. Rule90's no-reset spatial language remains the full shift. noreset_bulk.py.

On a fixed strict-east 12-site prefix with arbitrary incoming center AND independently refreshed exterior, forbid all rows whose 12 symbols reset the four old pairs. The temporal graph then has just two recurrent cycles, with center words 0 and 01011 up to rotation. Hence for *any* periodic mixed wall other than the second clock (and its rotations/repetitions), the gaps between these right-side resets are bounded in time. A hypothetical finite marked trajectory with the exceptional clock and aperiodic q also cannot stay in the graph forever, but this does not bound its gaps. A reset forces both previous center and x=1 for ALL possible pasts. At width 16 there can be at most 8,12,9,7 consecutive nonreset rows at the four requested clocks. If we only test previous *white* ticks, using their prescribed center=0 to intersect the pair mask with {00,10}, every 4,9,5,4 physical edges respectively contain a reconstructable white read from twelve following east cells; this is 7 for the exceptional 01011 clock. With a fully free center this latter selective test has a 1369-state variable component, so its four numerical bounds are not period-independent. Genuine R90 gives no such reconstruction. noreset_driver.py, noreset_wall.py, white_inverse_forks.py. Frequent reconstruction from a variable eastern word alone does not imply temporal periodicity of q.

The minimal free-wall width in this test is eight: widths 4–7 still contain variable recurrent SCCs; at eight the only recurring physical east words are 0 and the cycle 57→206→115→156→231→57. The corresponding masked read is one exactly at word 231 on these recurrent edges. The shared Step 4 draft now verifies this directly with a 4-bit rank per 8-site word. Every true noreset→noreset transition decreases rank or belongs to one of the six stated cycle edges. A tail with no resets has eventually constant rank, and therefore q eventually has period 5. This kernel-checked reduction applies to every wall and has [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound] as its whole axiom list. The remaining non-mosaic mixed case has cofinally many actual right8 resets as a proved premise; the final answer remains open. The genuine Rule90 four-pair action is always bijective, so right8 would never reset there while matched mixed clocks can have a non-5-periodic q.
