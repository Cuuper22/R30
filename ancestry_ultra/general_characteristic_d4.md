## The spatial conjugacy and its exact integer domain

Define J(u)[d]=H^{-d}(u)[d]. This is the original row-zero tape read leftward, when all strictly eastern row-zero sites are blank. It is an invertible triangular prefix map. It obeys JH=GJ for the normalized right-frame G, and H(2u)=2H(u), so J(2^P u)=2^P G^{-P}J(u). Thus an eventual period P is a finite difference between two complete H-orbits, u and its delayed counterpart 2^P u, with original exactly J(u)=1.

There is an exact domain calculation: J maps the nonzero ordinary signed integers bijectively onto precisely the west tapes with an eventually checkerboard tail. Indeed if the input u eventually equals 1 then the left neighbor is permanently 0; if u eventually equals 0 and has seen a pulse, its east neighbor eventually locks at 1. To see the latter, a finite rightmost pulse persists at every subsequent tick by rightward left permutivity, whereas a finite strict east initialized by such pulses and staying white at its first site under zero drive must be all white by induction across sites. Constant adjacent phases force the entire west half to be the same stationary checkerboard at a single cutoff: the west-recovery formula uses only that time and its successor. Earlier west tails stay checker: the spatial predecessor scan (a,b)→(y XOR(a OR b),a) on successive checker output bits synchronizes to the same checker after 4 or 5 digits according to initial phase. At most 4 cells are lost per reversed tick. For a temporal prefix of length K followed by zeros the east latch occurs by 2K, since the nearest strict-east mark by the stop is at distance at most K; for a suffix of ones use K. Hence a sufficient spatial checker-tail start in the row-zero tape is 8K (+ endpoint conventions).

Conversely a west checker ray ending at a black x is permanent irrespective of the east exterior. It advances at least two sites within 11 ticks. Until a=X(x+1)=0,b=X(x+2)=1 locks the next pair, the a-column must alternate: a'=NOT(a OR b), so every white tick has b=0. This is impossible for five successive white ticks, even freely controlling the exterior of four sites to the right of a.
