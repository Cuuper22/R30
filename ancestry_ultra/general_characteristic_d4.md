# Arbitrary periodic boundary: marked characteristic monodromy

Let W_i^s(j)=X(i+j+s,j−i)+2X(i+j+s+1,j−i), s=0,1. A site from the old row is (a,b) and the previous old upper bit is p. Put k=p OR a. To read the next row, update its previous packed state z by z' = sigma z+d mod 4, where sigma=+1 iff k XOR b, otherwise −1; d=3 when k, otherwise 2b. These equations follow separately on the two actual bits: u'=u XOR k, v'=v XOR (u' OR b). For the two-sided singleton both checkerboard starting states at j=−1 are known: z=2s,p=s. The remaining outer boundary is W_0^0(j)=3,1,3,1,... from the actual initially empty right half. Hence the entire center problem is the diagonal of iterates of this *one fixed* letter-to-letter transducer on a periodic right-edge input, with its starting state fixed by the left edge.

At j=i, t=2i+s, z is u+2v with u=C(t+2) XOR (C(t+1) OR R(t+1)), v=C(t+3) XOR (C(t+2) OR R(t+2)). Thus period-w observations give singleton constraints for every black neighbor tick, and a free offset controlled by the white-tick right observer otherwise. If C(t+2)=1, antipodal inputs to an identical old word cannot both pass the upper terminal test. No alignment parity is lost: the two checkboards use antipodal actual west anchors. For any old block J the sign of its net D4 permutation is (−1)^{|J| + sum_{J} b + u_end + u_start}, since k=u' XOR u. Apart from the endpoints the only additional monodromy datum is the old upper-bit parity; one must couple it to genuine marked evolution to recover white reads.

## Exact marked diagonal image and antipodal surgery

For a proposed full output row y_j=u_j+2v_j, put Δu=u_j XOR u_{j−1}, e_j=v_j XOR v_{j−1}. The only inverse constraints are b_{j−1} OR a_j=Δu_j and u_j OR b_j=e_j. Thus inverse choices factor *independently* into pairs (b_{j−1},a_j), plus the end bit b_m, or cyclically with no end bit. Existence at an interior output letter is exactly u_j ≤ e_j ≤ u_j OR u_{j+1}. At the even marked start u_0=v_0=0 and b_0=0, the first input lower bit sets u_1 freely. At the terminal output omit the upper bound involving u_{m+1}. For a fixed lower word U, the number of upper words is 2^{#01(U)+[u_m=0]}, since e_j is optional precisely before a rise, plus an optional terminal increment if u_m=0. Hence the complete marked image count is [1,1] [[1,2],[1,1]]^(m−1) [2,1]^T, with recurrence A_m=2 A_{m−1}+A_{m−2}. On a cyclic lower word the optional e values must XOR to XOR(U); without a rise the parity condition still applies. Upper phase has two choices when compatible. The two-sided singleton fixes that phase at its southwest anchor. For true R30 none of 61,254 internal triples violate the local image inequality; genuine whole-space R90 on 01,011,00101 violates 37,939 / 24,514 / 23,134, independently of the first anchor. `general_d4_inverse_product.py`.

In particular, for two 01 rises at j=p,q, flipping both optional increments flips the entire upper-bit interval [p,q), leaving every lower bit and both exterior phases intact. Some previous-row word realizing this twist agrees with the old previous-row word outside the two independent pairs (b_p,a_{p+1}),(b_q,a_{q+1}): toggle b at each end, set a=1 when b=0 requires it. This is a one-characteristic-layer inverse surgery, not yet a lift through the prescribed prehistory. Along the center the optional increment at a white tick is exactly the masked east neighbor, since Δv=C(t+1) XOR L(t)=C(t) OR R(t). The rise condition and temporal cyclic parity concern *one characteristic row*, not an entire time period of the center. Translating between the two is the open coupling.

For a period-p marked NE current row, its actual next row has period dividing 4p, the factor determined by the orbit of the starting group state under its D4 monodromy (the output may subsequently admit a smaller period).
