# P2 observer, true initialized support

Use a physical R30 field with X(0,x<0)=0 and X(0,0)=1; the initial right half is arbitrary. At every T>=1 the true strict west integer W, little-endian from x=-1, has bit length T. If C alternates starting at T, W advances by H(w)=(w div 2) xor (w or 2w). Its head is 1 on black ticks and 1 xor q on white ticks, where q is the adjacent east bit at that white tick. ## Near-square obstruction

For any nonzero finite W of length m under H, write h for its head trace. At lag k, the new outer mark is at bit m+k-1. At a fixed inward depth d, the lane from this mark obeys the normalized R30 transition A'(d)=A(d-2) xor (A(d-1) or A(d)) from a finite time on. Every finite lane prefix is eventually periodic. The periods of successively longer prefixes are unbounded powers of two: extending by one lane either resets when the previous lane is active or integrates with a one-bit state; bounded periods would yield a common P and a backward orbit of adjacent P-trace pairs leaving the fixed zero pair into the marked pair in a finite deterministic graph, impossible. At every record doubling lane j the previous lane j-1 is permanently zero after a finite time, since any active bit resets j once per old period. Lane j has a nonzero periodic trace, and j+1, driven by j-1=0, OR-latches permanently to 1. Consequently there are arbitrarily large fixed d at which the normalized bit of H^k(W) is 1 for all sufficiently large k.

At such d, H^k(W) disagrees with W at bit m+k-1-d, once k>d and this bit lies above W's support. The first differing head bit is no later, so k-LCP(h,h[k:]) tends to infinity. The same holds from every fixed start. Under P2 it also holds for the physical white-step q word (compare physical lags 2k). This is stronger than excluding ultimately periodic q. p2_observer_squares.py checks true rows and the nonlinear mark separator through 500. On the matched R90 example with original x0=1 and positive sites 2,4 mod6 black, C alternates forever, all normalized odd lanes vanish, and every positive even lane varies (the growing dyadic Pascal term driven by the initial mark has unique largest degree in each fixed lane).

## No finite-range additive bulk current

Let F be Rule30 on the entire spatial line and q>=1. A real density f on k+1 consecutive bits, with total on every periodic row invariant under F^q, has constant total per spatial site: it is constant modulo a spatial coboundary. Use torus circumference larger than, say, 4(k+2q)+4. Take the mixed finite difference of the invariant total in the two input bits -q and k+q. For k>=1 the original sum contains no term with both inputs. Exactly one term in the F^q total can involve both, namely f(Y_0,...,Y_k). Y_0 is permutive in the left variable and Y_k does not see it. Set the inputs in [k-q+1,k+q-1] to 0; Y_k now changes with the right variable, since the characteristic undergoes q consecutive white gates, and Y_0 does not see that variable. For prescribed Y_1,...,Y_{k-1}, fill their distinct extremal input bits i-q in descending order by left permutivity. Hence the endpoint mixed difference of f is zero for every interior word, so f splits as a function on its first k and another on its last k bits. Spatial summation reduces to width k. At width 1 the same light-cone extremes with zero interior give Y_0=left XOR right; its mixed difference forces f(0)=f(1). This proves the claim.

An additive one-step potential meant to slow the front uniformly on all initial right words must have its appropriate bulk change of one sign on periodic words (tile arbitrary periods arbitrarily many times away from the boundaries). The fair IID Bernoulli measure is invariant by left permutivity: for every finite target block, after the two rightmost source bits are fixed, the remaining inputs are uniquely solvable from right to left. The expected bulk change is zero. On the finite de Bruijn graph of the local change, all cycle sums have one sign; subtract a graph potential to make all edge weights have that sign. Full-support stationary uniform weights and mean zero force all adjusted weights zero. Thus bulk change has zero sum on *every* periodic word, to which the preceding theorem applies. A q-phase density gives the same conclusion upon composing q steps. This refutes phase-aware fixed-range additive **bulk** currents, not arbitrary nonlocal, ancestral, or boundary-dependent ranks; constant density plus boundary terms must still be distinguished from a genuine loss-rate bound.
