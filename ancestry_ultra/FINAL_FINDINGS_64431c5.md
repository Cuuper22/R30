## 8. Finite extinction test for the genuinely initialized cylinder

There is a precise alternative to demanding a whole steered white diagonal. Suppose an H-tick *spatially periodic* preimage P of global vacuum contains a black at 0, at least s whites immediately to its east, and its previous black at −D with D≥H+3. Crop P by putting vacuum strictly west of 0. This is a member of U_s. Its last discrepancy from P at birth is at −D; left permutivity makes the rightmost discrepancy at time H exactly H−D. The clipped row is then dark on all x≥−2. This contradicts the mature 3/5 strict west, for which W_1,W_2 are never simultaneously zero. One needs H in the mature comparison interval. The requirement is D just above H, not a blank cone of width 2H.

Conversely, this condition is necessary for *this particular target*. If u∈U_s has no black at x≥−2 after H, the right shift limits of u are H-tick full preimages of the vacuum. There are finitely many (at most 3^H by the Rule 30 preimage theorem; the elementary left-permutive bound 4^H already suffices). This finite set consists of spatially periodic rows. A one-sided word with finite periodic shift-limit set is eventually periodic: sufficiently long allowed words from those finitely many orbits have unique successor and distinct cycles cannot switch arbitrarily far out. Hence u has a periodic right donor P going to vacuum at H. P is nonzero, since an ordinary nonempty finite u has its rightmost black advance by H. The last discrepancy from P is M≤−H−3 by exact NE transport, and M is precisely the last P-black to the west of the initial mark. Thus P has the stated word and D=−M. An aperiodic east choice cannot evade this condition while accomplishing whole-east extinction.

There is no all-s construction of these paired gaps in the Rule 30 vacuum tower. Finite speed gives only the upper bound D≤2H for the spacing. One-sided density, independent control of the preceding gap and the eastern blank, and empirical gap growth would each be insufficient. On the kinematic Rule 90 control, a single ring mark at period 2H with H a power of two vanishes at H because the two H-shifts coincide. Its rightward truncation leaves precisely one black at −H. The clipping argument is kinematic; the freedom of all initial eastern completions coupled to a jointly nonwhite true west pair is the nonlinear Rule 30 premise. Rule 90 with the genuine nonfinite alternating eastern seed does not have this protective cylinder. This does not substitute an additive singleton for the alternating control.
